## What this does *not* cover

This licence applies only to material written for this project. It does not
and cannot extend to:

- **The books themselves.** Each remains the copyright of its author and
  publisher. This repository contains no book text, and the site hosts no book
  files. Per-book attribution is on the site's copyright page.
- **Linked course material.** Lectures, problem sets, and solutions stay under
  their own licences on their own servers. MIT OpenCourseWare material, for
  example, is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. We link to it; we do not redistribute it.
- **Book structure.** Chapter and section titles are quoted as factual
  reference — the minimum needed to point a reader at the right place in a
  book they own or are studying.

## For contributors

By contributing prose you agree to publish it under CC BY-SA 4.0, and you
confirm it is your own writing rather than a reproduction of the book's text,
worked examples, or exercises.

Short attributed quotes are fine. Closely paraphrasing a whole section is not:
the purpose of a guide is commentary that sends people to the source, not a
replacement for it.
